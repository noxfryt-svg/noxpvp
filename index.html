      The tier system

      Six tiers, each split into Low, Mid and High. That gives 18 ranks per gamemode, from LT6 at the bottom to HT1 at the top. Tier 1 is the strongest.

      Gamemodes

      Each gamemode has its own tier. A player can be HT1 in Crystal and LT4 in Axe.

      Crystal

      End crystals, obsidian and totems. Timing decides everything.

      Sword

      Diamond sword duels with golden apples. Clean combos win.

      NethPot

      Netherite and splash potions. Manage health and effects under pressure.

      Mace

      Wind charges and smash attacks. High risk, high reward.

      Axe

      Shield breaks and heavy hits. Patience beats panic.

      Get your tier

      Tiers are earned in a live test against a ranked tester.

      Step 1

      Join the Discord

      Open the tier test channel and pick your gamemode.

      Step 2

      Enter the queue

      Post your Minecraft name and region. A tester calls you when it is your turn.

      Step 3

      Fight your test

      Play a short first-to-score match. Your result sets your starting tier.

      Step 4

      Show up on the board

      Your tier appears in Rankings. Retest after a cooldown to move up.
